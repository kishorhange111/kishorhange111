### Hi, I'm Kishor 👋

I'm a Software Engineer at **E2open** in Bengaluru, building production ML and GenAI systems. I've spent 3+ years taking models from experiment to production: LLM apps and RAG, fine-tuning, inference optimization and the MLOps around them.

I care most about the engineering around the model: making it fast, measurable and reliable once real users depend on it.

- **LLM support chatbot** that automatically resolves customer support tickets
- **RAG pipelines** on LangChain with FAISS and Chroma, evaluated with Ragas and DeepEval for faithfulness, relevance and context precision
- **Agentic workflows** with LangChain and CrewAI, plus **MCP** integrations that give LLMs secure access to enterprise databases
- **LLM fine-tuning** with PEFT (LoRA, QLoRA) for domain-specific and multilingual use cases
- **38% lower inference latency** through quantization and pruning with ONNX Runtime and Hugging Face Optimum
- **9.5% higher accuracy** from replacing a legacy text classifier
- **Production monitoring** with FastAPI, Airflow, Prometheus and Grafana to catch model performance drops
